### 1. Introduction
eSIM Coach participates in various affiliate programs that provide a means for us to earn a small commission when visitors purchase products or services through links on our site.

### 2. How Affiliate Links Work
When you click on an affiliate link and make a purchase, we may receive a commission from the provider at no extra cost to you. This helps support our research and maintenance of the site.

### 3. Our Commitment to Transparency
We only recommend providers and plans we genuinely believe are useful and relevant.  
Affiliate relationships **do not** affect our editorial independence or ranking methodology.

### 4. No Influence on Reviews or Rankings
Affiliate partners cannot pay for preferential placement or modify our editorial content.  
We base recommendations on plan performance, pricing, coverage, and user experience.

### 5. Compliance with UK/EU Regulations
We follow the **UK Competition and Markets Authority (CMA)** guidance and the **Committee of Advertising Practice (CAP) Code**.  
All affiliate links and sponsored content are clearly marked.  
Users are informed whenever a recommendation contains an affiliate link.
