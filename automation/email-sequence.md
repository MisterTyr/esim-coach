## Email 3 — The savings playbook (Day 7)

**Subject:** Three ways travellers overpay for data
**Preview:** And the cheaper move each time.

Hi {{first_name}},

Three common ways to overpay abroad — and what to do instead:

1. **Carrier roaming.** Convenient, usually the priciest. A prepaid travel eSIM is often a fraction of the cost.
2. **Buying too much data.** Maps and messaging sip data. For most trips a mid-size plan is plenty; top up if you run low.
3. **One country at a time.** On multi-country trips, a regional plan (all of Europe or Asia on one eSIM) beats buying a new plan at each border.

We rank plans by real value — price per GB, or per day for unlimited — so you can see the cheap options at a glance:
→ [Compare plans by value](https://esim.coach/)
