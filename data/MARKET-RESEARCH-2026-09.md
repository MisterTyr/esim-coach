# eSIM Provider Market Research

## UK Travellers Abroad & Travellers Coming to the UK

**Research date:** 3 September 2026

## Executive Summary

The international eSIM market is now crowded, but most of the headline
brands are fundamentally **data providers**. Airalo, Holafly, Nomad,
Saily, Ubigi, GigSky, Jetpac and similar companies collectively cover
200+ countries in many cases, but conventional voice and SMS remain
relatively unusual.

There are really **three product classes**, not one:

1.  UK mobile networks selling roaming-enabled eSIM/PAYG
2.  International travel-eSIM specialists
3.  Destination-network tourist eSIMs such as Orange Travel or Vodafone
    Travel

For comparison purposes, data-only products should be kept distinct from
plans that genuinely include **voice + SMS**.

The strongest integrated **data + voice + SMS** propositions identified
are currently:

-   Orange Travel
-   Airalo Discover+
-   Some Nomad destination products
-   Saily's Global plan with phone-number add-on
-   Roamless
-   Three UK PAYG

For travellers coming into Britain, there are effectively five competing
product types:

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Market                              Typical proposition
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  International travel eSIM           Buy online before travelling;
                                      usually data only

  UK travel eSIM                      EE/Vodafone-style visitor product

  UK PAYG eSIM                        Proper UK number + data + calls +
                                      SMS

  International full-service travel   UK connectivity + number/calls/SMS
  eSIM                                

  VoIP hybrid                         Mobile data plus an app-based phone
                                      number/calling service
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

This distinction is highly relevant to **eSIM.coach**, because most
comparison sites lump these products together even though they are not
directly equivalent.

Airalo divides its catalogue between **Data** and **Data / Calls /
Texts**. Its global Discover+ packages include allowances such as 1GB +
10 minutes + 10 SMS for seven days through to 20GB + 200 minutes + 200
SMS valid for 365 days.

Roamless combines a global eSIM, data, purchasable phone numbers,
conventional international calls and SMS. Credits do not expire and the
eSIM works in 200+ destinations.

Saily remains data-only on normal country and regional packages, but
offers a phone-number add-on with its Global plan.

Ubigi remains explicitly data-only.

Holafly is predominantly an unlimited-data proposition rather than a
voice/SMS service.

# 2. Europe

Europe is one of the strongest territories for a UK traveller who wants
**data + calls + SMS**.

## Orange Travel

Orange Travel is a standout.

Its Europe eSIM covers more than 40 European territories and offers
plans containing:

    Validity    Data Voice       SMS
  ---------- ------- ----------- -----------
     14 days    20GB Unlimited   Unlimited
     30 days    50GB Unlimited   Unlimited
     30 days   100GB Unlimited   Unlimited
     30 days   200GB Unlimited   Unlimited
     90 days   500GB Unlimited   Unlimited

Current pricing has included approximately €24.99 for 20GB/14 days,
although promotions change frequently.

Coverage includes Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia,
Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland,
Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands,
Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden,
Switzerland and numerous smaller European territories.

The UK itself is also included, making the plan useful for UK + EU
multi-country trips.

### Other strong European choices

Airalo offers individual European country eSIMs and regional packages,
with selected packages supporting calls and SMS.

Roamless is attractive for travellers moving repeatedly between
countries because one eSIM remains installed and works across its global
footprint.

Three UK PAYG is a major alternative. Its PAYG eSIM Data Packs include
roaming allowances across more than 70 destinations. Within Go Roam in
Europe, active Data Packs permit unlimited calls to UK and eligible
European destinations plus unlimited SMS.

O2 PAYG is another option. Its plans include roaming within more than 45
European destinations, with up to 25GB data plus calls and texts.

# 3. United States

The US is one of the most competitive travel-eSIM destinations.

For pure data, Rewild Mobile, Airalo, Nomad, Saily and others compete
heavily on price.

For customers requiring **voice + SMS**, the landscape changes.

## Orange Travel USA

Orange connects travellers to networks including AT&T, T-Mobile and
Verizon, alongside other roaming partners. Its US catalogue includes
fixed-data and unlimited packages, with eligible plans advertising calls
and SMS.

## Airalo

Airalo's global Discover+ product works particularly well for customers
who want the same voice-capable service across the US and subsequent
countries.

## Roamless

Roamless supports US data alongside its integrated calling/SMS/number
system, avoiding the need to buy a fresh eSIM for each destination.

For UK customers whose domestic network charges a daily roaming fee, an
independent travel eSIM can be considerably cheaper.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Canada

Canada has broadly the same provider landscape as the US.

Orange Travel operates through local partners and offers packages
advertising data plus calls/SMS options.

Airalo's North America regional package is particularly relevant for
customers combining the US and Canada.

Roamless eliminates the US/Canada boundary problem because its global
eSIM automatically changes networks as the traveller crosses countries.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Australia and New Zealand

Australia is highly competitive on data.

A July 2026 Which? comparison found:

  Requirement   Competitive Australia option
  ------------- ------------------------------
  1GB           Rewild
  5GB           Rewild
  10GB          Rewild
  Unlimited     Nomad

Orange Travel also operates an Australian product and markets eligible
packages with calls/SMS as well as data.

Airalo, Roamless and Saily all cover Australia/New Zealand, although the
exact voice proposition depends on the plan.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Japan

Japan is a mature eSIM market.

Orange Travel connects through KDDI, NTT Docomo, Rakuten and SoftBank,
giving broad host-network coverage. Its Japanese product advertises
data, calls and SMS.

For pure data, a July 2026 comparison found Rewild particularly
competitive at 1--10GB, while Saily was competitive for unlimited usage.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. China

China deserves its own category because internet routing and censorship
make provider choice more important than price alone.

A July 2026 comparison found Rewild particularly competitive for
1--10GB, while Nomad supplied one of the cheaper unlimited options.

Airalo, Nomad, Saily, Ubigi, Roamless and other major travel-eSIM
operators serve China.

For calls/SMS, Airalo's voice-enabled global package or Roamless are
conceptually more useful than standard data-only Chinese travel eSIMs.

A future comparison database should include a separate field for
**internet routing / Great Firewall bypass behaviour**, because
nominally identical Chinese eSIMs can provide materially different
experiences.

# 8. Thailand and Southeast Asia

Thailand is one of the cheapest eSIM destinations.

A July 2026 comparison found approximately:

         Data Competitive option
  ----------- --------------------
          1GB Saily
          5GB Saily
         10GB Nomad
    Unlimited Nomad

Regional Asia-Pacific plans become particularly compelling for
travellers combining Thailand, Vietnam, Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia,
Japan and other destinations.

Nomad's Asia-Pacific regional product was around £15.90 for 10GB/30 days
when checked.

The same regional logic applies to Airalo, GigSky, Jetpac, Saily and
Ubigi.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. Indonesia

For a ten-day trip, a July 2026 comparison found Rewild particularly
competitive at 1GB and 10GB while Nomad performed strongly at 5GB and
unlimited.

Most of these are data-only.

For actual telephone functionality, travellers should look toward
**Roamless, Airalo voice-enabled plans or other destination products
explicitly stating calls/SMS**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 10. UAE

The UAE is another major UK outbound destination.

A July 2026 comparison found Rewild competitive across 1GB, 5GB and
10GB, with Nomad competitive for unlimited usage.

VoIP availability and local restrictions matter in the UAE. Therefore
"includes WhatsApp calling" should not be treated as equivalent to
conventional cellular voice service.

A database should distinguish:

-   Native cellular voice
-   Provider/app VoIP calling
-   Third-party VoIP compatibility

These are materially different services.

# 14. Caribbean, Latin America and Africa

Major international platforms increasingly offer regional rather than
only country-specific products.

Airalo provides regional products covering:

-   Europe
-   Asia
-   Caribbean
-   Latin America
-   Africa
-   Middle East/North Africa
-   North America
-   Oceania
-   Global

GigSky similarly sells regional Europe, Asia, Africa and Caribbean
packages and has carved out an unusual niche through **Cruise + Land**
connectivity.

Jetpac sells regional packages and differentiates itself with
travel-related features such as airport lounge access following eligible
flight delays.

The Latin America market is comparatively expensive. A July 2026
comparison found an Airalo regional package around £28.50 for 10GB/30
days.

# 15. UK Network Alternative

There is another market that conventional travel-eSIM comparison sites
tend to overlook.

## Three PAYG Travel eSIM

Three effectively sells a travel eSIM to UK consumers.

Current PAYG Data Packs include:

    Price     UK data   Roaming allocation
  ------- ----------- --------------------
      £10        40GB                  6GB
      £15       100GB                 12GB
      £20       200GB                 18GB
      £35   Unlimited                 30GB

All work in 70+ Go Roam destinations.

Within Europe the product includes unlimited qualifying calls and texts;
in Go Roam Around the World destinations, customers receive up to 3,000
minutes and 3,000 texts back to the UK.

That is a strong proposition if voice/SMS matters.

## O2

O2 PAYG offers eSIMs and Europe Zone roaming with up to 25GB roaming
data.

Current £10 PAYG includes 10GB and unlimited UK minutes/texts; larger
packages offer 30GB, 50GB and 150GB.

O2 says its Europe Zone contains more than 45 destinations.

Outside Europe, the proposition becomes less attractive unless the
customer is on an eligible Pay Monthly tariff or bolt-on.

# PART II: TRAVELLERS COMING TO THE UK

This is arguably the more interesting market commercially.

There are now **proper UK visitor eSIMs, domestic PAYG eSIMs and
international UK travel eSIMs** competing for the same customer.

# 16. EE Travel eSIM

EE sells a purpose-built **UK Travel eSIM** aimed at visitors.

Published packages include:

        Stay Data        Calls   SMS     Price
  ---------- ----------- ------- ----- -------
    \~7 days Unlimited   No      No        £15
     14 days Unlimited   No      No        £20
     30 days Unlimited   No      No        £30
     90 days Unlimited   No      No        £90

EE describes it explicitly as a **data-only SIM**.

No UK address is required and activation can occur before arrival.

This makes it simple, but unsuitable for tourists requiring a genuine UK
telephone number.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 17. Vodafone Travel eSIM

Vodafone Travel operates a dedicated UK visitor eSIM.

Plans range from 2GB to unlimited, with 5G where available and hotspot
capability.

The travel product is primarily positioned around mobile internet rather
than replacing a full conventional UK mobile subscription.

Therefore Vodafone Travel eSIM and Vodafone UK PAYG should be catalogued
separately.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 18. O2 PAYG eSIM

For a visitor who wants a proper UK mobile service, O2 PAYG is
particularly interesting.

Current O2 PAYG offers are available as either physical SIM or eSIM.

Examples include:

    Price    Data UK calls    UK SMS
  ------- ------- ----------- -----------
      £10    10GB Unlimited   Unlimited
      £15    30GB Unlimited   Unlimited
      £20    50GB Unlimited   Unlimited
      £30   150GB Unlimited   Unlimited

Some plans also include international minutes.

For an overseas visitor who wants a real **+44 UK mobile number**, this
is much closer to what many people intuitively mean when asking for a UK
eSIM.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 19. Three PAYG eSIM

Three supports PAYG eSIM and provides a genuine UK mobile number, data,
calling and texting.

Its large data allowances make it particularly attractive to longer-stay
tourists.

An additional advantage is that the visitor can subsequently travel from
Britain into Europe and retain some roaming functionality through Go
Roam.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 20. Vodafone UK PAYG

Vodafone has a full domestic PAYG service with conventional calls, texts
and data plus optional international calling extras.

Its standard PAYG proposition includes unlimited calls/texts on days
when the £2 daily PAYG allowance is triggered, alongside a small data
allowance; additional data and international-call packages can be
purchased separately.

This should be catalogued separately from Vodafone Travel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 21. Lebara UK

Lebara is particularly interesting for visitors who also need to call
home.

Its UK packages run on Vodafone's network and combine data, UK minutes
and SMS. Its positioning specifically emphasises international minutes
on eligible plans.

Lebara supports eSIM, although device and activation eligibility can be
more restricted than some competitors.

For immigrants, students, business travellers and people visiting
family, Lebara's proposition can be better aligned than a conventional
tourist data eSIM.

# 24. Nomad UK With Calls and SMS

Nomad has started offering UK voice products.

Its August 2026 material describes UK packages starting around **US\$13
for 5GB + 100 calling minutes + 100 SMS**, valid for 30 days, alongside
cheaper conventional data plans.

This is worth monitoring.

Historically Nomad was overwhelmingly a data-eSIM service, so the
addition of genuine voice-capable products suggests the wider
travel-eSIM market is moving towards full mobile-service replacement.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 25. aloSIM

aloSIM takes a different approach.

Its UK eSIM includes mobile data plus a **free Hushed phone number** for
calls/texts.

This should be classified differently from Orange/O2/Three because the
telephone service is not necessarily native cellular voice provision
tied directly to the serving SIM.

From a consumer perspective, however, it may solve the same problem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 26. Roamless UK

Roamless is one of the more technologically interesting entrants.

Its UK product uses the same global eSIM platform as elsewhere, while
the app provides:

-   Data
-   Purchasable numbers
-   Calls
-   SMS

The customer can therefore arrive in Britain without changing the
installed eSIM.

For frequent travellers rather than occasional holidaymakers, this is a
strong proposition.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 27. Ubigi UK

Ubigi sells extensive UK data packages including unlimited options, but
explicitly states that it is **data only**.

Customers retain their existing SIM/number for conventional calling
while using WhatsApp, FaceTime, Teams and other apps over Ubigi data.

One current UK unlimited package offers **25GB full-speed usage over
seven days before optimisation to 2Mbps**, while the 30-day unlimited
product provides **60GB at full speed before dropping to 2Mbps**.

This fair-use detail matters when comparing products claiming
"unlimited."

This research indicates that **country + price + GB is nowhere near

## Recommended Voice Type taxonomy

Use:

1.  **Native cellular**
2.  **App-integrated VoIP**
3.  **External VoIP only**
4.  **None**

This single classification could differentiate eSIM.coach from many
existing comparison sites.

# Key Commercial Insight

The best "travel eSIM" is increasingly **not necessarily a travel
eSIM**.

For someone coming to Britain for 30 days who needs a UK number, calls
and texts, a £10--£20 domestic PAYG eSIM may be vastly more appropriate
than a £20 international data-only travel package.

Conversely, someone travelling through six European countries does not
want six local PAYG accounts. They want Orange, Airalo, Roamless or a
regional equivalent.

That creates a much more intelligent comparison proposition for
**eSIM.coach** than simply sorting hundreds of 10GB bundles by price.

## Source notes

Key public sources used in the research include Which?'s 2026
travel-eSIM comparisons and provider information from Airalo, Orange
Travel, Roamless, Saily, Ubigi, Three, O2, EE, Vodafone, Lebara, Nomad
and aloSIM.

Prices, allowances, coverage and voice/SMS inclusions are dynamic and
should be independently re-verified before being entered into a
production comparison database.
