### 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how **eSIM Coach** (“we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, and protects your personal information in compliance with the **UK GDPR** and the **EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**.

### 2. Information We Collect
We may collect:
- Contact information (e.g. your name and email address when you subscribe to our newsletter)  
- Usage data (such as pages visited, time on site, and browser type via analytics tools)  
- Technical data (IP address, device type, cookies)

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data or data from children under 16.

### 3. How We Use Your Information
We process data to:
- Provide and maintain our website  
- Send newsletters or updates when you opt-in  
- Analyse site usage to improve performance  
- Comply with legal obligations

### 4. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)
We rely on the following lawful bases:
- **Consent** – for newsletter subscriptions and cookies.  
- **Legitimate interest** – for analytics and website operation.  
- **Legal obligation** – when required to retain or disclose information by law.

### 5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies
We use cookies to enhance your experience, analyse traffic, and deliver relevant content. You can manage or disable cookies in your browser settings. Non-essential cookies are only set with your consent.

### 6. Data Retention
We keep personal data only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined here or to meet legal obligations.  
Newsletter subscriber data is retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion.

### 7. Your Rights
Under GDPR, you have the right to:
- Access, correct, or delete your data  
- Withdraw consent at any time  
- Object to or restrict processing  
- Data portability  

You can exercise these rights by contacting us at **hello@esim.coach**.

### 8. Security
We take reasonable measures to protect your data using encryption, secure hosting, and limited access controls. No online transmission is 100% secure, but we strive to maintain the highest standards of protection.

### 9. Third-Party Services
We may use third-party tools (e.g. Google Analytics, MailerLite) that process data on our behalf. Each provider operates under its own privacy policy compliant with GDPR.

### 10. International Data Transfers
If data is transferred outside the UK or EEA, we ensure appropriate safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

### 11. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
